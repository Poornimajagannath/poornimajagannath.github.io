Visa Acceptance Platform (VAP) is a high-trust payment platform. People and agents integrate with it when the next step is clear.

I lead the PM team for developer experience on that platform. I set the direction with them. We decide what to take on, what to leave alone, and how to write the path so someone else can follow it. The work is shared. I do not treat a team result as my own solo work.

Three things matter to me here. Clear writing. Good systems. Developer trust.

Two pieces of that work are written up separately. [A first successful test payment](/work/visa-acceptance-platform) is the path. [Docs a person or an agent can follow](/work/agent-ready-docs) is how I check that a guide can be followed.

Status: ongoing. This is how the work is led, not a single launch.
