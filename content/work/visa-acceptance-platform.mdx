Developers, partners, and AI agents need the same thing at the start. They need a first successful test payment.

That path was too slow and too hard to follow. A person has to find the right API, pick an SDK, set up a sandbox, and make a call that works. An agent has to do the same thing from the docs.

I lead the product work for that path on Visa Acceptance Platform (VAP). It covers the developer center, the APIs, the SDKs, the docs, sandbox setup, and the agent tools that came later. I do this with a team. I set the direction. I do not do it alone.

Status: shipped, and still changing.

- [Cybersource Developer Education Series](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL9qINLsWlhs2fzbdsdEBTMNgasNhbG4er). A video series I hosted on payments and the developer center.
